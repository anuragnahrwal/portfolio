Questions

FAQ

What types of videos do you edit?+
I specialize in long-form YouTube videos, short-form reels and shorts, and brand/promotional content. I'm comfortable with talking-head content, vlogs, tutorials, and cinematic edits.
How long does it take to edit a video?+
Turnaround depends on length and complexity. A 5–10 minute YouTube video typically takes 2–3 days. Short-form reels are usually done within 24 hours. I'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.
What formats do you accept?+
I accept all standard formats — MP4, MOV, MKV, and more. You can share footage via Google Drive, WeTransfer, or any cloud platform. I'll handle the rest.
Do you offer revisions?+
Yes — every project includes revisions. I want you to be 100% happy with the final output. We'll keep iterating until it feels exactly right.
How do I get started?+
Just drop me an email at editoranurag135@gmail.com or DM me on Instagram. Tell me about your project and we'll take it from there. Simple as that.
