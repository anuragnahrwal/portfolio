FAQ

What types of videos do you edit?
Long-form YouTube videos, short-form reels and Shorts, brand/promotional content. Comfortable with talking-head, vlogs, tutorials, and cinematic edits.
How long does it take to edit a video?
A 5–10 min YouTube video typically takes 2–3 days. Short-form reels are usually done within 24 hours. I'll always give a clear timeline upfront.
What formats do you accept?
All standard formats — MP4, MOV, MKV, and more. Share footage via Google Drive or WeTransfer. I'll handle the rest.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes — revisions included with every project. I want you 100% happy. We'll keep iterating until it feels exactly right.
How do I get started?
Email me at editoranurag135@gmail.com or DM on Instagram. Tell me about your project — we'll take it from there.
