About Me

What I do

Background

Mechanical engineering student at DTU, Delhi — but my real passion lives in the edit suite. My engineering background gives me a structured approach: I think about pacing, retention, and psychology. Why do viewers drop off? What makes them rewatch?

Achievements

Edited several short films that won 1st place at multiple competitions — bringing the same storytelling instincts I use for YouTube to narrative cinema.
